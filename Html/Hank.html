Story

Hank is a robot who was supposed to be discarded, but turns out he was useful. Hank is a Robot Bunny who wields a Hammer. he has spring limbs, in which help him jump higher. His creator is Eggman. He is still pretty much a Badnik, just more evolved as a regular one. He faces Amy mainly, but sometimes Sonic.

Damged Hank: Hank after being damaged a lot, which resulte in him losing both arms and his hammer, he still has his other limbs though, and should not be underestimated.

Very Damaged Hank: Here, he is SO damaged that fire literally surrounds him as he can practically Self-Destruct at any moment. He still will fulfill his order, no matter what.
