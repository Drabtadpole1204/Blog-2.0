Alex

Deer / Side-Character

Story

Alex is a young deer who aims to be a Sannin when he grows up. He is 12 years old.

At his teenage years, Alex became a Shinobi, one step closer to his dream. He is 17 years old.

Now an adult, Alex has already faced many threats and beasts, but there is still a long way for him to go to make his dream come true. He is 38 years old.

As a Sannin, Alex protects his home from threats like the ones he faced younger, he is 60 years old.

Fun Fact: This OC is more or less a crossover between Naruto and the Sonic.
