Paradigm

Robot/ Side Character

Story

From another one of my first AUs, Professional!Tale.

Abilities

  • Blue Soul: Its Telekinesis.
  • Reality Cannon: Paradigm can turn his right hand into a hand cannon/blaster, in which can destroy nearby realities if they are not strong enough. This is one of his strongest moves.
  • Code Manipulation: Can change/manipulate code of those who have it, this doesn't have any effect against who has scrambled code or is above Paradigm's power level.
  • Laser Eyes: He can shoot laser out of his eyes.
  • Passive Abilities:
  • Flight.
  • Super-speed.
  • Regeneration/Self-repair.
  • Super-Strength.
  • Super-Jump.
  • Data Copy: Can copy data/information from his opponents and store it, so he can use their attacks for example.
  • Auto-Aim: He automatically aim at enemies when he uses ranged moves, attacks, or weapons.

AU (Alternate Universe)

He belongs to Professional!Tale, an AU where Dr.Voidheim (This AU's Gaster) founded the Dominion Academy, which will be explained another time, anyways, Paradigm was created as a self-aware robot, he used to play and take care of Voidhem's children, Sub X and Sub Y, after they grew up, he started to do things much bigger.

  • Fun Fact: Paradigm's name is ironic as the pattern for robots is to not feel feelings, being able to eat,drink,etc. Paradigm can do those.
