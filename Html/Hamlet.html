Story

Hamlet The Knight, a fierce and brave Knight who has never lost a duel in his life, he is also known as Hamlet, The Undefeated. He never reveals his face.

Broken Mask Hamlet: His mask was broken after he met his match, he still does not reveal his face, he keeps it covered at all the times which it is possible.

Unbeatable Knight 50%: Hamlet calls this state Unbeatable Knight because he uses every trick he has up his sleeve. Here, he only uses half of it, or less.

Unbeatable Knight: The Unbeatable Knight state was unleashed when a beast with unscalable power threatened to destroy the world, and Hamlet was NOT going to let that happen. He uses every trick he has up his sleeve, and more.
