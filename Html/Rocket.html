Rocket T.Chark

Antagonist

Story

Rocket is a hybrid between a Chicken and a Shark, that is why he is a Chark, he also pretends to be a good guy, he owns a casino, and his goal is to get his hands on the phantom ruby, for power, and flex it, for fame.

He accquires the Phantom Ruby at some point, and modified it so it could be in his Gem Cuffs, like the ones Niles the Bathog has.

50% of the Ruby's Power: He is figuring out how to unleash the power of the Phantom Ruby fully, but his main focus is to get rid of Niles and his friends, who are meddling with his plans.

100% of the Ruby's Power: Rocket has figured out how to use the Ruby's full extent of its power, and is consumed by power, fame, and his desire to get rid of a certain group of children meddling with his plans. But his blinding-desires could be his downfall.
