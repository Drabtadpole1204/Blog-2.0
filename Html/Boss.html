Omniversal Boss

Villain

Story

Omniversal Boss is the first boss who will try to stop the player from progressing in Test Ops, and try to undo the Omniversal Black Hole the player creates in the game.

Abilities

  • Super-Endurance: Self-explanatory
  • Super-Speed: Self-exaplanatory
  • Super-Strength: Self-explanatory
  • Void: Creates a void that damages players that touuch it.
  • Sword Barrage: Creates a barrage of Omniverse Enchanted Swords.
  • Eye of the Omniverse: Summons a lot of eyes that can see shoot very big and powerful beams.

AU

Omniversal Boss appears in Test Ops as the first boss, he is very powerful and can take on multiple players at once. His name is Omniversal Boss because he is one of the bosses/leaders of the Omniverse. Although he is not the weakest among them, he is still very powerful and a force to be reckoned with.
