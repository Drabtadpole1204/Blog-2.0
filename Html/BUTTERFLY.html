B.U.T.T.E.R.F.L.Y

Villain/ Robot/ Experiment

Story

The Ultimate Robot, created by the same creator as X's, Dr. Samuel Blacklight, who is an animal from the same species as Infinite. This robot went rogue and follows his own code, his main goal is to take 'Fix' the world, and he will destroy anyone who gets in his way.

Abilities

  • Electric Discharge: Releases a Electric discharge that can stun and hurt enemies.
  • Spindash: Spindash like Sonic's.
  • Home-Jump: Home-Jump like Sonic's.
  • Chaos Control: Can use Chaos Control like Shadow and Sonic, but he does not need a Chaos Emerald.
  • Passive Abilities:
  • Super-Speed: Self-explanatory.
  • Super-Strength: Self-explanatory.
  • Super-Durability: Self-explanatory.
  • Super-Endurance: Self-explanatory.
