Raku The Knight

Hero/ Side-Character

Story

Raku is a basically a knight but modern, he's a hedgehog aswell, he helps Sonic and his team fight Eggman and other threats, Raku uses Knight Equipment and Rollerblades, in which he made, he also wields a Sawblade, also made by him.

Abilities

  • Weapon: A Sawblade.
  • Spindash: Spindash like Sonic's.
  • Home-Jump: Home-Jump like Sonic's.
  • Super Kick: Kicks the enemy so strong they fly for a couple of seconds, the kick hurts more because of the Rollerblades.
