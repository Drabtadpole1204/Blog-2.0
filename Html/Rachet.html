Rachet The Deer

From Side Antagonist to Hero

Information

Rachet is one of Eggman's many creations, not the first that was discarded and forgotten, but certainly not the last. He has a 'Deer Mode' in which he becomes more agile, but it became useless overtime so it is not used anymore.

Abilities

  • Homejump: A Homejump like Sonic's.
  • Equipment:
  • Katana: Its the weapon he uses for self-defense and attack in his base form.
  • Passive Abilities:
  • Super-Speed: Self-explanatory.
  • Super-Strength: Self-explanatory.
  • Can Block Spindashes with his antlers and much more.

Story

Rachet is a scrapped robot deer made by Eggman, who now protects the scrapped robots after escaping Eggman's base, he was made to get rid of Sonic, whose spindash and homejump he can block with his antlers. He has a Super Form and a Hyper Form. In which can be obtained after getting the 7 Chaos Emeralds and the 7 Super Chaos Emeralds, respectively.

He met Sonic once, Rachet was very aggressive and they fought a lot, but after some time, Rachet became an ally. He still protects other discarded robots from Eggman.
