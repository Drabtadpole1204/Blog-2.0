END 404

Death Agent / Force

Story

Once, The Author realized there were too many OP 404s in Creatorverse, so he decided to do something about it, he took END 666 as a reference, created a body that ressembled him and colored it, named it and gave it a purpose, and so END404 was born. END 404 is like Black Flash, he is Death itself to 404s, as they are many and some are OP, so END 404 visits them when their time has come. END 404 also is not limited to Creatorverse as his 'job' applies to EVERY 404, not only in Creatorverse.

The stronger the 404 is, the stronger END 404 becomes.

Fun-Fact: I was originally going to name him 'Inverted END' but it didn't make sense, and I thought of 'Begginning 555' but it didnt fit much, I named him END 404 because he ressembles Error 404 due to the White and Blue colors. He was first introduced in Test Ops.

Fun-Fact: END 404's 'mouth' is actually an eye.

Abilities

  • Blue Soul: Telekinesis. Its force depends on the 404.
  • Teleportation: Can teleport to any location, this isnt limited to Creatorverse.
  • END 404 Blaster(s): Gaster Blasters with one eye. Their power depends on the strength of the 404.
  • END 404 Bone(s): Literally bones. Their power depends on the strength of the 404.
  • END 404 Eye: A single eye that can see every 404, it is not limited to Creatorverse.
  • Existence Erasure: Self-explanatory, this only applies to 404, and it bypasses traditional immunity, it does not work on beings with Polar Immortality, Conceptual Immortality, or even ones that cannot be erased by their own creator.
  • Immortality: Although he is immortal, his immortality ends and he dies once every 404 is gone, and if he dies and another 404 is created, he will come back.
  • Passive Abilities:
  • END-Speed: END 404 is fast enough to catch any 404, no matter the speed.
  • END-Force: END 404 is atleast double or triple the stronger the 404 he faces is.
  • END-Durability: END 404 is atleast double or triple the durability of the 404 he faces is.

More

END 404 is death for 404s only. he can be seen by other entities but cannot be harmed by them in the traditional way, he also has immunity to erasure, except by The Author's. END 404 only 'visits' 404s when their time has come, as stated earlier, he keeps every 404 check.
