Story

Tony is liquid life form taking the form of a Male Echidna, he is a villain pretending to be a good guy, currently trying to become the mayor of Station Square.

Determined Tony: At this point, he was already discovered by Niles the Bathog and his friends, now he, the 'Determined' state gives him a boost on every aspect, though, it is temporary.

The Dealer: Tony is also known as The Dealer, who was an unknown entity that made deals with people, giving them what they wanted in exchange for their loyalty. He tries to persuade Niles and his friends. A fun fact is that Tony is a alien liquid life form.

He is not holding back anymore. Giving it his all.
