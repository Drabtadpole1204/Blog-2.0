Niles The Bathog

Protagonist/ Hero/ Hybrid

Story

Niles is an hybrid between a Bat and a Hedgehog, in his Gem Cuffs he can store Chaos Energy and use it, though he needs to learn how to control his power, he is 10 years old.

Niles' Super Form: Can be transformed into once Niles has enough Chaos Energy.

Niles' Dark Form: It is a form that is unleashed in the same way as Sonic's Dark Form, in Nile's Dark Form, the Chaos Energy in his cuffs get 'corrupted' the longer he is on this form. By 'corrupted' , it means that the Chaos Energy becomes Negative Chaos Energy.

Nile's Hyper Form: Can be transformed into after Niles masters his powers of Chaos Energy and turn them into Super Chaos Energy, which allows him to tap into the power of the Master Emerald and turn into Hyper Niles. Niles can only turn Chaos Energy into Super Chaos Energy every once in a while.

Abilities

  • Spindash: A Spindash like Sonic's.
  • Homejump: A Homejump like Sonic's.
  • Chaos Control: Can use Chaos Control like Shadow and Sonic, but he does not need a Chaos Emerald. Its better than Rachet 2.0's Chaos Control.
  • Chaos Manipulation: Can manipulate Chaos Energy.
  • Passive Abilities:
  • Super-Speed: Self-explanatory.
  • Invulnerability (Super and Hyper Form): Invulnerable in Super Form, Hyper form is more durable than Super form, Nile's Super Form goes on par with Rachet 2.0's.

More
