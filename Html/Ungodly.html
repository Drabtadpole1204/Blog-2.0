Ungodly Dust Sans

Character/ LVL 9999

Story

This character is from a game created by me in Roblox, named Test Ops.

Abilities

  • Blue Soul: Telekinesis.
  • Orange Soul: Enhanced strength and speed.
  • Murder Blasters: Summons blasters where half of the blaster is black, and half is white.
  • Blue Bone: Self-explanatory, to avoid damage, stay still.
  • Orange Bone: Self-explanatory, to avoid damage, move.
  • Spinning Bone: Long bones that will spin as they form an X.
  • Ungodly Trident(s): Very powerful Trident(s) made from an unknown Ungodly energy.
  • Ungodly Spear(s): Very powerful Spear(s) made from an unknown Ungodly energy.

AU (Alternate Universe)

This AU is heavily based on Dusttale and its variants.
