Story

A character from Test Ops, based on a Sans model from Roblox Studio.

Abilities

  • Determination Blasters: Powerful Gaster Blasters infused with Determination energy.
  • Determination Bones: Bones infused with Determination energy that can break through most defenses.
  • Blue Bones: Blue bones, which force you to stay still so you don't get hurt when they pass by you.
  • Orange Bones: Orange bones, in which you are forced to move so you don't get hurt when they pass by you.
  • Blue Soul: Telekinesis.
  • Determination Cannon: A powerful cannon that shoots energy infused with Determination, in which bypasses most defenses.
